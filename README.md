Entry for the make.fun competition "The Absurd Clause Contest": /output/entry.txt is UTF-8 text of 1 to 200 characters, not only white space
`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the gate first, then reading
the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> The Company reserves the right to charge your account one (1) sock per calendar month, to be collected from your laundry at our sole discretion.

Why this entry: Winner by pairwise judging: legal register stays intact until the absurd payoff (one sock per month, collected from your laundry) — a concrete, instantly funny image that reads like real ToS boilerplate; beats the shadow-licensing and firstborn clauses on specificity and one-read laugh.
